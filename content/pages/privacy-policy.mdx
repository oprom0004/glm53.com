## Who operates GLM-5.3 AI

GLM-5.3 AI at **glm53.com** is operated by **KIROOAI INC**. Contact [contact@glm53.com](mailto:contact@glm53.com) about this policy or your information.

## Information used to provide the service

- **Account:** your name, email address, profile image where provided, sign-in provider identifier, and authentication records. Google sign-in supplies the profile information you authorize; it does not give GLM-5.3 AI your Google password.
- **Creation:** prompts, uploaded images and reference videos, generation settings, task results, generated videos, and credit usage records.
- **Billing:** order and subscription identifiers, purchase amounts, payment status, and related Stripe customer identifiers. Card entry is handled by Stripe on its checkout pages; GLM-5.3 AI does not receive or store your full card number or security code.
- **Operation:** request and error logs, browser and network information, and cookies needed for sign-in, security, and preferences. Additional analytics depend on the features enabled on the site.

We use this information to authenticate users, generate and store videos, show creation history, manage credits and subscriptions, address support requests, investigate errors or abuse, and meet applicable obligations.

## Service providers and media links

Our service uses Google for optional sign-in, Stripe for payment processing, Vercel for hosting, Neon for the database, Cloudflare R2 for file storage, and configured AI providers such as Kie for generation. Relevant prompts and input media are sent to the provider processing your request. These providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies and may process information in other countries.

Media is delivered using file URLs that can be accessed by someone who obtains the link. Treat these links as shareable, and avoid uploading confidential information or another person's media without permission. Account access restrictions do not make a copied media link private.

## Retention and requests

Account, task, media, and billing records are retained as needed to operate the service, provide purchased benefits, resolve disputes, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Retention can differ between data types and providers; we do not promise an automatic deletion deadline for all records.

You may contact support from your account email to request access, correction, or deletion, or to raise a privacy concern. We may verify account ownership and explain any information that must be retained. Rights and applicable exceptions depend on your location. Canceling a subscription stops renewal; it does not by itself delete your account or videos.

## Updates

Changes will appear on this page with an updated effective date. Where additional notice or consent is required, it will be provided as required. See also our [Terms of Service](/terms-of-service).
